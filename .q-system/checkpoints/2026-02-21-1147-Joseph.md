### UB Research Results (massive, ~50 passages across Papers 4, 18, 20, 21, 34, 43, 53, 54, 109, 110, 116, 119, 120, 127, 128, 134, 136, 158, 176, 186, 187, 193, 196)

Key UB threads found:

1. **The Requirement:** Creator Sons take a solemn oath to the Trinity not to assume full sovereignty until seven creature bestowals are completed and certified by the Ancients of Days (119:0.3).

2. **The Purpose:** To become wise, sympathetic, just, understanding sovereigns. The Ancients of Days "will never certify a Creator Son as Universe Sovereign until he has really acquired the viewpoint of his own creatures by actual experience" (119:0.6).

3. **He Could Have Skipped It:** "A Creator Son could assert full sovereignty at any time, but he wisely chooses not to" (21:3.2). "The Michaels choose to experientially *earn* it" (21:3.3).

4. **The Seven Bestowals (Paper 119):**
   - 1st: As a Melchizedek Son (united will of Father, Son, Spirit)
   - 2nd: As a Lanonandek Son/System Sovereign (will of Father and Son)
   - 3rd: As a Material Son/Adam (will of Father and Spirit)
   - 4th: As a Supreme Seraphim (will of Son and Spirit)
   - 5th: As an ascending spirit mortal on Uversa (will of Infinite Spirit)
   - 6th: As a morontia mortal (will of Eternal Son)
   - 7th: As Jesus of Nazareth, mortal of the flesh on Urantia (will of Universal Father)
   - Total span: almost one billion years of Urantia time (119:8.2)

5. **The Mortal Task on Urantia:**
   - "Earning the right to become sovereign... the unquestioned and supreme ruler" (127:0.4)
   - "Earning the right of unqualified rulership" (128:1.1)
   - Used no supernatural power, "employed no agency not a part of his human endowment" (128:1.3)
   - Traversed the seven psychic circles "by the force of his own decisions" (134:8.4)
   - Mount Hermon: "finished the mortal task of achieving the circles of mind-understanding and personality-control" (134:8.4)
   - Mount Hermon: "Michael of Nebadon won the unquestioned sovereignty of his universe" (134:8.9)
   - Defeated Lucifer and Caligastia as "an unaided mortal of the realm" (136:3.1)

6. **Sovereignty Formally Declared:**
   - Constellation Father: "The sovereignty of Michael number 611,121 over his universe of Nebadon rests in completion... assume your well-earned unconditional rulership of all Nebadon" (136:3.5)
   - He was free to leave at any time but chose to continue
   - At Transfiguration: declined to be invested with full authority, chose to continue mortal life (158:1.4)

7. **"All Power in Heaven and on Earth":**
   - "All power in heaven and on earth was relegated to this triumphant and enthroned Master Son" (21:4.5)
   - Before bestowals: "He had not yet been vested with 'all power in heaven and on earth'" (53:5.2)
   - Creative Mother Spirit subordinated herself, giving him jurisdiction over "all power in heaven and on earth" (33:2.3)

8. **Nature of Earned Authority:**
   - "His power is unlimited since derived from experienced association with Paradise Deities; his authority is unquestioned inasmuch as it was acquired through actual experience in the likeness of universe creatures; his sovereignty is supreme" (120:0.5)
   - "One who has fully earned the right to rule a universe and administer its worlds" (21:4.6)

9. **The Lucifer Rebellion Connection:**
   - Before bestowals, Michael could not exercise summary jurisdiction over Lucifer (53:5.2, 54:5.6)
   - Immanuel counseled him to end the rebellion "as the Son of Man" rather than by creator power (120:2.2)
   - Mount Hermon victory "virtually settled" the Lucifer rebellion (134:8.9)

10. **"It Is Finished":**
    - "It was literally finished. His death on Urantia completed his bestowal career; it was the last step in fulfilling the sacred oath of a Paradise Creator Son" (21:4.5)

### ACIM Research Results

Key Course passages found:

1. **"All Power" from Decision (T 5 D 9):** "My mind will always be like yours, because we were created as equals. It was only my DECISION that gave me all power in Heaven and earth. My only gift to you is to help you make the same decision FOR YOURSELF."

2. **Model for Decision (T 5 D 10):** "I am your model for decision. By deciding for God, I showed you that this decision CAN be made, and that YOU can make it."

3. **Completed as a Man (T 4 G 15):** "I have told you several times that I am in charge of the whole Atonement. This is ONLY because I completed my part in it as a man, and can now complete it through other men."

4. **My Lesson Was Like Yours (T 4 B 11):** "My lesson was like yours, and because I learned it I can teach it."

5. **Nothing You Cannot Attain (T 1 B 40y):** "There is nothing about me that you cannot attain. I have nothing that does not come from God. The main difference between us as yet is that I have NOTHING ELSE."

6. **Equal as Learners (T 5 D 1):** "It is the final lesson that I learned, and God's Sons are as equal as learners as they are as Souls."

7. **I Learned for You (T 15 D 10):** "It is BECAUSE I learned for YOU, that you can learn of ME."

8. **In Charge of Atonement (T 1 B 23e):** "I am in charge of the process of Atonement, which I undertook to begin."

9. **In Charge of Second Coming (T 4 E 18):** "I am in charge of the Second Coming as I have already told you."

10. **All Power Given (T 14 G 2, T 26 G 2):** Two more references to "all power" given to the Son.

11. **Note:** Jesus never uses the word "earned" in the Course. He says "my DECISION" gave him power, he "completed my part as a man," and "my lesson was like yours."
