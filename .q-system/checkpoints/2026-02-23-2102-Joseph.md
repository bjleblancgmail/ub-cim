### 4. Punctuation as Source of Trend (Key Discovery)
- Created `/tmp/acim_punct_test.py`
- **CRITICAL FINDING:** The upward trend is entirely created by punctuation placement
  - ACIM Text: With punct +4.3%, No punct -2.4% (REVERSES)
  - ACIM Workbook: With punct +5.8%, No punct -2.1% (REVERSES)
  - Moby Dick: With punct -0.9%, No punct -0.3% (both flat)
- The words themselves become slightly LESS pentameter-friendly later
- The punctuation increasingly places clause breaks at 10-syllable positions

### 5. Alternative Punctuation Strategies
- Created `/tmp/acim_punct_alternatives.py`
- Tested 8 different punctuation strategies on same text
- Original punctuation: 20.4% pentameter (avg across samples)
- Breath pauses (natural phrasing): 22.6% (similar!)
- Conjunctions: 13.7%, Sentences only: 9.1%
- **Key insight:** Natural breath pauses produce similar pentameter, meaning the WORDS suggest where the commas go, and those natural pause points happen to be at pentameter positions
- Example passage: Original punct = 9/15 pentameter, conjunctions = 2/15, breath = 3/15
- Conclusion: punctuation isn't artificial; the word choices create natural phrases that land at 10 syllables
