# Appendix B: Selected Parallels: Urantia Book and Course in Miracles

*57 parallels between The Urantia Book and A Course in Miracles, organized into 10 subject groups.*

---

## The Thought Adjuster: God Within

---

### 1. The Divine Spark / Adjuster

Both texts use the same word: a divine "spark" that God has kept alive within every mind. It cannot be extinguished, and faith releases its power.

Course in Miracles: "The power of one mind *can* shine into another, because all the lamps of God were lit by the same SPARK. It is everywhere, and it is eternal." (acim oe txt 9.X.91)

Course in Miracles: "In many only the SPARK remains, for the Great Rays are obscured. Yet God has kept the SPARK alive so that the rays can never be completely forgotten." (acim oe txt 9.X.92)

Course in Miracles: "If you but see the little SPARK, you will learn of the greater light, for the rays are there unseen. Perceiving the SPARK will heal, but knowing the light will create." (acim oe txt 9.X.92)

Urantia Book: "Faith acts to release the superhuman activities of the divine SPARK, the immortal germ, that lives within the mind of man, and which is the potential of eternal survival. The human soul of man survives mortal death by identity association with this indwelling SPARK of divinity, which is immortal." (UB 132:3.6)


---

### 2. Constant Communication with God

Both texts use the word "constant" to describe the communication link God placed within us. The only variable is whether we are listening.

Course in Miracles: "The Communication Link which God Himself placed within you, joining your minds with His, *cannot* be broken... the sweet and CONSTANT COMMUNICATION which God would share with you." (acim oe txt 13.V.42)

Urantia Book: "And indirectly and unrecognized the Adjuster is CONSTANTLY COMMUNICATING with the human subject, especially during those sublime experiences of the worshipful contact of mind with spirit in the superconsciousness." (UB 110:1.1)


---

### 3. Loneliness of God / Adjuster

Course in Miracles: "His joy is not complete because *yours* is incomplete... HE IS LONELY when the minds He created do not communicate fully with Him." (acim oe txt 4.VIII.100)

Urantia Book: "They truly and divinely love you; they are the prisoners of spirit hope confined within the minds of men. THEY LONG FOR THE DIVINITY ATTAINMENT OF YOUR MORTAL MINDS THAT THEIR LONELINESS MAY END." (UB 107:6.2)

---

### 4. Mind Wandering / Deaf to Spiritual Pleas

The Adjuster is speaking, but we are not listening. Not because the signal is weak, but because our minds are undisciplined.

Course in Miracles: "You are much too tolerant of mind wandering, thus passively condoning its miscreations." (acim oe txt 2.IV.75)

Urantia Book: "So few mortals are real thinkers; you do not spiritually develop and discipline your minds to the point of favorable liaison with the divine Adjusters. The ear of the human mind is almost deaf to the spiritual pleas which the Adjuster translates." (UB 110:7.6)


---

### 5. Your Creations / Adjuster Duplicates

The Course says our creations are protected by the Holy Spirit. The Urantia Book reveals the mechanism: the Adjuster duplicates every worthy mental creation with a spiritual counterpart. Nothing of spiritual worth is ever lost.

Course in Miracles: "Your creations are protected *for* you, because the Holy Spirit, Who is in your mind, knows of them and can bring them *into* your awareness whenever you will let Him." (acim oe txt 7.X.95)

Urantia Book: "These faithful custodians of the future career unfailingly duplicate every mental creation with a spiritual counterpart; they are thus slowly and surely re-creating you as you really are." (UB 108:6.5)

Urantia Book: "Adjusters work in the spheres of the higher levels of the human mind, unceasingly seeking to produce morontia duplicates of EVERY CONCEPT of the mortal intellect." (UB 110:2.4)


---

### 6. "What Is the You?" / The Real You

The Course asks: what is the "you" who is living in this world? The Urantia Book answers with specificity: the Adjuster is the divine parent of "the real you," your higher and advancing self.

Course in Miracles: "WHAT IS THE 'YOU' WHO ARE LIVING IN THIS WORLD? IMMORTALITY IS A CONSTANT STATE. It is as true now as it ever was or ever will be because it implies *no change at all*." (acim oe txt 4.VII.81)

Urantia Book: "And as you are the human parent, so is the Adjuster the divine parent of THE REAL YOU, your higher and advancing self, your better morontial and future spiritual self." (UB 108:6.6)


---

### 7. Soul = Adjuster Substitution

Try reading the Course's statements about the "soul" with "Adjuster" substituted. They fit perfectly. When the Course says "soul," it is often describing exactly what the Urantia Book calls the Thought Adjuster.

In these Course in Miracles quotes The "Adjuster" is substituted for "Soul":
God and the souls (Adjusters) He created are completely dependent on each other." (acim oe txt 2.II.20)
The soul (Adjuster) never loses its communion with God." (acim oe txt 1.I.45)
Souls (Adjusters) are perfect creations and experience awe only in the Presence of the Creator of perfection." (acim oe txt 1.I.77)
The soul (Adjuster) yearns to share its being as its Creator did." (acim oe txt 7.X.93)
Each soul (Adjuster) knows God completely." (acim oe txt 3.VII.59)

Urantia Book: "Spirit identification constitutes the secret of personal survival and determines the destiny of spiritual ascension. And since the Thought Adjusters are the only spirits of fusion potential to be identified with man during the life in the flesh." (UB 40:5.4)


---

### 8. Appeal to the Spirit Within

You cannot put the Spirit into someone. You can only recognize the Spirit that is already there and reawaken it. The Urantia Book gives the same instruction directly from Jesus: appeal to the divine spirit that already dwells within the minds of men.

Course in Miracles: "You do not put the Spirit in them by inspiring them because that would be magic and therefore would not be real healing. You do, however, recognize the Spirit that is already there and thereby reawaken it." (acim oe txt 7.V.31)

Urantia Book: "Make your appeals directly to the divine spirit that dwells within the minds of men. Do not appeal to fear, pity, or mere sentiment. In appealing to men, be fair; exercise self-control and exhibit due restraint; show proper respect for the personalities of your pupils." (UB 159:3.2)


---

### 9. God Gives Everything

Both texts describe a God who holds nothing back. The Course says real creation gives everything because it can only create like itself. The Urantia Book says the Father has divested himself of every part of himself that was bestowable.

Course in Miracles: "God, Who encompasses *all* being, nevertheless created beings who have everything individually but who want to share it to increase their joy... REAL CREATION GIVES EVERYTHING, since it can create *only* like itself." (acim oe txt 4.VIII.99)

Urantia Book: "THE UNIVERSAL FATHER ALL ALONG HAS DIVESTED HIMSELF OF EVERY PART OF HIMSELF THAT WAS BESTOWABLE ON ANY OTHER CREATOR OR CREATURE... HE HAS GIVEN AWAY, actually bestowed, with the dignity and sanctity of personality possession, ALL OF HIMSELF AND ALL OF HIS ATTRIBUTES, EVERYTHING HE POSSIBLY COULD DIVEST HIMSELF OF." (UB 10:1.2)

## God the Supreme: The Whole

---

### 1. The Circle of Creation

Both texts reach for the same geometry to describe infinity. The Course calls it "the circle of creation" with no beginning and no end. The Urantia Book calls it "an almost limitless ellipse" produced by one absolute cause.

Course in Miracles: "THE CIRCLE OF CREATION HAS NO END. Its starting and its ending are the same. But in itself it holds the universe of all creation, without beginning and without an end." (acim oe txt 28.III.16)

Urantia Book: "The human mind may helpfully approach the problem by conceiving eternity-infinity AS AN ALMOST LIMITLESS ELLIPSE which is produced by one absolute cause, and which functions throughout this universal circle of endless diversification." (UB 105:0.1)


---

### 2. Returning Home / Finding the Supreme

Both describe spiritual attainment not as arriving somewhere new but as remembering something we already knew. The destination is not unfamiliar. It is the most familiar thing there is.

Course in Miracles: "Listen and see if you remember an ancient song you knew so long ago and held more dear than any melody you taught yourself to cherish since." (acim oe txt 21.II.9)

Urantia Book: "The Supreme is your universe home, and when you find him, it will be like returning home." (UB 117:6.1)


---

### 3. Simultaneous Discovery of the Supreme/Sonship

No one finishes alone. The Course says the Atonement will be completed when all talents are shared by all the Sons of God. The Urantia Book says no single ascender will find the Supreme until all ascenders are ready simultaneously.

Course in Miracles: "When the Atonement has been completed, *all* talents will be shared by *all* the Sons of God." (acim oe txt 1.I.88)

Urantia Book: "No single ascender will ever find the Supreme until all ascenders have reached that maximum universe maturity which qualifies them simultaneously to participate in this discovery." (UB 117:6.21)


---

### 4. Joint Will of the Sonship / God the Supreme

The Course calls it the joint will of the Sonship. The Urantia Book calls it the discovery of the Supreme. The whole must arrive together, because the whole is the point.

Course in Miracles: "The full power of creation cannot be expressed as long as *any* of God's ideas withhold it from the Kingdom. The joint will of *all* the Sonship is the only creator that can create like the Father, because only the complete can think completely." (acim oe txt 5.VI.57)

Course in Miracles: "We are the joint will of the Sonship, whose wholeness is for all." (acim oe txt 8.VI.42)

Urantia Book: "No single ascender will ever find the Supreme until all ascenders have reached that maximum universe maturity which qualifies them simultaneously to participate in this discovery." (UB 117:6.21)


---

### 5. Universes Virtually Nonexistent to the Father

The Course says the world as you perceive it was not created by the Father. The Urantia Book says the universes are virtually nonexistent to the Father; he deals with personalities. The material world is not the point. Personality is.

Course in Miracles: "The world as you perceive it cannot have been created by the Father, for the world is not as you see it. God created only the eternal." (acim oe txt 10.VIII.69)

Urantia Book: "AS FAR AS the Universal Father is concerned, as a Father, the universes are virtually nonexistent; he deals with personalities; he is the Father of personalities." (UB 15:0.1)


---

## Jesus: Elder Brother and Sovereign

---

### 1. Elder Brother and Sonship

Urantia Book: "Elder Brother" and "Sonship" appear nowhere in the Bible but are central to both texts. The Course uses them as spiritual terms. The Urantia Book confirms them as cosmic facts.

Course in Miracles: "I bridge the distance as an ELDER BROTHER to man on the one hand and as a Son of God on the other. My devotion to my brothers has placed me in charge of the SONSHIP." (acim oe txt 1.I.80)

Course in Miracles: "An ELDER BROTHER is entitled to respect for his greater experience and a reasonable amount of obedience for his greater wisdom." (acim oe txt 1.I.78)

Urantia Book: "A Creator Son of God became one of you; he is your ELDER BROTHER in fact." (UB 40:6.5)

Urantia Book: "You have accepted the status of SONSHIP. You believe in the reality of your SONSHIP, and thus does your SONSHIP with God become eternally real." (UB 40:6.4)


---

### 2. Mind of Jesus / Holy Spirit

Both texts present the mind of Jesus as something available right now, not remote or unreachable. The Course identifies it with right-mindedness. The Urantia Book offers it as a direct exchange for our confused thinking.

Course in Miracles: "The Holy Spirit is nothing more than your own right mind. HE WAS ALSO MINE. The Bible says, 'May the mind be in you that was also in Christ Jesus.'" (acim oe txt 5.II.9)

Urantia Book: "Even on Urantia, these seraphim teach the everlasting truth: If your own mind does not serve you well, you can exchange it for the MIND OF JESUS of Nazareth, who always serves you well." (UB 48:6.26)


---

### 3. Illusion of Loneliness / Personality Isolation

Both texts connect loneliness directly to fear, and both say Jesus is the answer to it. We live on a quarantined world where the isolation feels real, but the indwelling spirit means we were never truly alone.

Course in Miracles: "I am come as a light into a world that *does* deny itself everything... It is therefore an illusion of isolation, maintained by fear of the same loneliness which *is* its illusion. IF I AM WITH YOU IN THE LONELINESS OF THE WORLD, THE LONELINESS IS GONE." (acim oe txt 8.V.26)

Urantia Book: "Jesus is now revealing to the worlds the final triumph over all fears of creature personality isolation... the SPIRITUALLY ISOLATED AND COSMICALLY LONELY MORTALS OF EARTH ARE ENABLED TO ESCAPE PERSONALITY ISOLATION, with all its consequences of fear." (UB 184:4.6)


---

### 4. Assault Can Only Be Made on the Body

The body is real enough in time, but it is not the real you. What is real about you cannot be attacked.

Course in Miracles: "ASSAULT CAN ULTIMATELY BE MADE ONLY ON THE BODY. There is little doubt that one *body* can assault another and can even destroy it. YET IF DESTRUCTION ITSELF IS IMPOSSIBLE, THEN ANYTHING THAT IS DESTRUCTIBLE CANNOT BE REAL." (acim oe txt 6.II.7)

Urantia Book: "But I say to you, my friends, when they seek to destroy the Son of Man, be not afraid of them. Fear not those who, ALTHOUGH THEY MAY BE ABLE TO KILL THE BODY, AFTER THAT HAVE NO MORE POWER OVER YOU." (UB 165:3.3)

Urantia Book: "BE NOT AFRAID OF THOSE WHO CAN KILL THE BODY, BUT WHO ARE NOT ABLE TO DESTROY THE SOUL." (UB 150:4.2)


---

### 5. Jesus Earned His Sovereignty

Jesus' authority was not inherited. It was earned through choice and experience. The Urantia Book tells the story behind the decision the Course describes.

Course in Miracles: "IT WAS ONLY MY DECISION THAT GAVE ME ALL POWER IN HEAVEN AND EARTH. My only gift to you is to help you make the same decision *for yourself*." (acim oe txt 5.IV.27)

Urantia Book: "On an afternoon in late summer, amid the trees and in the silence of nature, MICHAEL OF NEBADON WON THE UNQUESTIONED SOVEREIGNTY OF HIS UNIVERSE." (UB 134:8.9)

Urantia Book: "THE SOVEREIGNTY OF MICHAEL NUMBER 611,121 OVER HIS UNIVERSE OF NEBADON RESTS IN COMPLETION." (UB 136:3.5)


---

## Forgiveness, Love, and Sin

---

### 1. Forgiveness Already Accomplished

God's forgiveness is not something we earn. It already exists. Our only task is to extend it to others.

Course in Miracles: "ASK NOT TO BE FORGIVEN, FOR THIS HAS ALREADY BEEN ACCOMPLISHED. Ask, rather, to learn how to *forgive* and restore what always was to your unforgiving mind." (acim oe txt 13.IX.88)

Urantia Book: "THE FATHER IN HEAVEN HAS FORGIVEN YOU EVEN BEFORE YOU HAVE THOUGHT TO ASK HIM, but such forgiveness is not available in your personal religious experience until such a time as you forgive your fellow men." (UB 146:2.4)


---

### 2. Lack of Love as the Problem

Neither text treats sin as something to punish. Both treat it as a lack to be filled. The response to a lack is not condemnation but supply.

Course in Miracles: "AS SOON AS YOU REGARD SIN AS A LACK YOU WILL AUTOMATICALLY ATTEMPT TO REMEDY THE SITUATION. And you will succeed." (acim oe txt 5.VII.64)

Urantia Book: "THE LESS OF LOVE IN ANY CREATURE'S NATURE, THE GREATER THE LOVE NEED, and the more does divine love seek to satisfy such need." (UB 156:5.11)


---

### 3. Forgiveness Destroys Sin vs. Pardon Preserves It

Both texts draw the same distinction between two kinds of forgiveness. Pardon keeps the record of guilt. Love absorbs and actually destroys it.

Course in Miracles: "To witness sin and yet forgive it is a paradox which reason cannot see. For it maintains what has been done to you deserves no pardon. And by giving it, you grant your brother mercy but retain the proof he is not really innocent." (acim oe txt 27.III.15)

Urantia Book: "Divine love does not merely forgive wrongs; it absorbs and actually destroys them. The forgiveness of love utterly transcends the forgiveness of mercy. Mercy sets the guilt of evil-doing to one side; but love destroys forever the sin." (UB 188:5.2)


---

### 4. Turn the Other Cheek: Active Response

Both texts reject the idea that spiritual response means doing nothing. The response to attack is not weakness but a deliberate, active choice to respond from a higher perspective.

Course in Miracles: "My one lesson, which I must teach as I learned, is that no perception which is out of accord with the judgment of the Holy Spirit can be justified." (acim oe txt 6.II.15)

Urantia Book: "He even opposed negative or purely passive nonresistance. Said he: 'When an enemy smites you on one cheek, do not stand there dumb and passive but in positive attitude turn the other; that is, do the best thing possible actively to lead your brother in error away from the evil paths.'" (UB 159:5.9)


---

## Free Will and Personality

---

### 1. Free Will Cannot Be Overridden

Free will is the one thing the universe will never override. The Course says God himself would not go against it. The Urantia Book says no being, force, or agency in all creation can interfere with it to any degree.

Course in Miracles: "MY WILL CANNOT OVERCOME YOURS, BECAUSE YOURS IS AS POWERFUL AS MINE. If it were not so, the Sons of God would be unequal... YOUR WILL IS AS FREE AS MINE, AND GOD HIMSELF WOULD NOT GO AGAINST IT." (acim oe txt 8.V.31)

Urantia Book: "THE CREATOR REFUSES TO COERCE OR COMPEL THE SUBMISSION OF THE SPIRITUAL FREE WILLS OF HIS MATERIAL CREATURES." (UB 1:1.2)

Urantia Book: "NO OTHER BEING, FORCE, CREATOR, OR AGENCY in all the wide universe of universes CAN INTERFERE TO ANY DEGREE WITH THE ABSOLUTE SOVEREIGNTY OF THE MORTAL FREE WILL." (UB 5:6.8)


---

### 2. Limits on Free Will / Creation Limits

Free will is sovereign but not unlimited. We can delay, wander, and even rebel, but we cannot permanently destroy ourselves or the plan. The guardrails are built into creation.

Course in Miracles: "Everything is limited in some way by the manner of its creation. FREE WILL CAN TEMPORIZE AND IS CAPABLE OF ENORMOUS PROCRASTINATION. BUT IT CANNOT DEPART ENTIRELY FROM ITS CREATOR, WHO SET THE LIMITS ON ITS ABILITY TO MISCREATE." (acim oe txt 2.II.47)

Urantia Book: "MECHANISMS PRODUCED BY HIGHER MINDS FUNCTION TO LIBERATE THEIR CREATIVE SOURCES BUT TO SOME DEGREE UNVARYINGLY LIMIT THE ACTION OF ALL SUBORDINATE INTELLIGENCES." (UB 118:9.2)


---

### 3. Adjusting Self to Other Selves

You cannot grow toward God without also growing toward your brothers. The Course describes the psychological mechanism. The Urantia Book states the spiritual principle.

Course in Miracles: "Each man makes one ego for himself, although it is subject to enormous variation because of its instability, and one for everyone he perceives, which is equally variable. Their interaction is a process which literally alters both." (acim oe txt 4.III.25)

Urantia Book: "While religion is exclusively a personal spiritual experience (knowing God as a Father), the corollary of this experience (knowing man as a brother) entails the adjustment of the self to other selves." (UB 99:5.1)


---

## The Mind: Perception and Knowledge

---

### 1. Conflict Is Evil / Civil War in Personality

Both texts treat internal conflict not as a normal part of growth but as something that actively blocks it. Peace is the condition required for the soul to create.

Course in Miracles: "Conflict and peace are opposites. Where one abides the other cannot be; where either goes the other disappears." (acim oe txt 23.II.18)

Urantia Book: "All conflict is evil in that it inhibits the creative function of the inner life: it is a species of civil war in the personality." (UB 111:4.11)


---

### 2. Two Thought Systems / Fringe of Conflict

The pain comes not from either reality alone but from trying to serve both at once. Choose one and relinquish the other.

Course in Miracles: "The way *out* of conflict between two opposing thought systems is clearly *to choose one* and *relinquish the other.* If you *identify* with your thought system... and if you accept two thought systems which are in *complete* disagreement, peace of mind *is* impossible." (acim oe txt 6.III.75)

Urantia Book: "Forewarn all believers regarding the fringe of conflict which must be traversed by all who pass from the life as it is lived in the flesh to the higher life as it is lived in the spirit. To those who live quite wholly within either realm, there is little conflict." (UB 159:3.7)


---

### 3. Soul Cannot Perceive / Beyond Intellectual Comprehension

The ego mind cannot reach spiritual reality by thinking harder. It requires a different faculty entirely.

Course in Miracles: "The Soul cannot perceive and the ego cannot know. They are therefore *not in communication* and can never *be* in communication." (acim oe txt 4.II.8)

Urantia Book: "The realities of religion are utterly beyond the mortal capacity for intellectual comprehension." (UB 5:5.6)


---

### 4. No Private Thoughts

We may believe our thoughts are hidden, but they are not. When you realize you cannot hide, you stop trying, and that is where fear ends.

Course in Miracles: "Today we are again emphasizing the fact that minds are joined... it is a fact that there are no private thoughts." (acim oe wrk 19.2)

Urantia Book: "The real nature of any service, be it rendered by man or angel, is fully revealed in the faces of these secoraphic service indicators... Mortals may employ words to conceal their thoughts, but these high seconaphim lay bare the deep motives of the human heart." (UB 28:6.19)


---

### 5. Worship as Identification with the Whole

The ego contracts. Worship expands. The Course is teaching us to stop fencing off a small self and start identifying with the Whole.

Course in Miracles: "The little that the body fences off *becomes* the self, preserved through sacrifice of all the rest... If we see ourselves as one self, we will find our Self." (acim oe txt 26.II.3)

Urantia Book: "Worship is the act of a part identifying itself with the Whole." (UB 143:7.8)

Urantia Book: "Worship is self-forgetting, superthinking." (UB 143:7.7)


---

### 6. Holy Spirit/Vision Replaces Judgment

You do not have to figure everything out with your own judgment. The Course calls the alternative vision. The Urantia Book calls it the mind of Jesus. The offer is the same: stop guessing and start listening.

Course in Miracles: "Judgment will always give you false directions, but vision shows you where to go. Why should you guess?" (acim oe txt 21.II.4)

Course in Miracles: "There is another vision and another Voice in which your freedom lies awaiting but your choice." (acim oe txt 21.VI.51)

Urantia Book: "If your own mind does not serve you well, you can exchange it for the mind of Jesus of Nazareth, who always serves you well." (UB 48:6.26)


---

### 7. Do Thoughts Occupy Space?

Both texts ask the same startling question: do thoughts occupy space? The Course says no. The Urantia Book's revelators confess they do not know, but are sure an idea pattern does not contain space. That a celestial being of high order admits uncertainty tells you something about the depth of the question.

Course in Miracles: "Ideas do not *leave* the mind which thought them to have a separate being, NOR DO SEPARATE THOUGHTS CONFLICT WITH ONE ANOTHER IN SPACE BECAUSE THEY DO NOT OCCUPY SPACE AT ALL." (acim oe txt 5.VI.56)

Urantia Book: "DOES THE PATTERN (THE REALITY) OF AN IDEA OCCUPY SPACE? We really do not know, albeit we are sure that an idea pattern does not contain space." (UB 118:3.7)


---

### 8. The Spiritual Eye

Both texts describe a faculty of perception that is not physical. The Course calls it the Spiritual eye. The Urantia Book calls it seeing with the eyes of the spirit. Neither is speaking metaphorically.

Course in Miracles: "Corrective learning always begins with the awakening of the Spiritual eye and the turning away from the belief in physical sight... the Spiritual eye cannot see error and is capable only of looking beyond it to the defense of Atonement." (acim oe txt 2.III.67)

Urantia Book: "And so finding yourself born of the spirit and happily in the kingdom of God, you would begin to bear in your daily life the abundant fruits of the spirit... very soon would you begin to see with the EYES OF THE SPIRIT." (UB 142:6.7)


---

### 9. Levels of Thought: Meaning and Values

Both texts describe a layered hierarchy of consciousness. The Course maps it as perception, understanding, knowledge. The Urantia Book maps it as fact, meaning, value. Both agree that perception is the starting point, not the summit.

Course in Miracles: "Understanding *is* beyond perception because it introduces meaning. It is, however, below knowledge even though it can grow towards it." (acim oe txt 5.VI.44)

Urantia Book: "Mortal consciousness proceeds from the fact, to the meaning, and then to the value. Creator consciousness proceeds from the thought-value, through the word-meaning, to the fact of action." (UB 118:5.3)


---

### 10. Circle Attainment / Adjutant Influence Diminishes

As you advance, you increasingly defer to a higher source of guidance. The Course calls it asking the Teacher. The Urantia Book calls it circle attainment, where adjutant influence progressively diminishes.

Course in Miracles: "As the teacher of God advances in his training, he learns one lesson with increasing thoroughness. He does not make his own decisions; he asks his Teacher for His answer, and it is this he follows as his guide for action." (acim oe mnl 9.2)

Urantia Book: "From the seventh to the third circle there occurs increased and unified action of the seven adjutant mind-spirits in the task of weaning the mortal mind from its dependence on the realities of the material life mechanisms... From the third circle onward the ADJUTANT INFLUENCE PROGRESSIVELY DIMINISHES." (UB 110:6.20)


---

## What Is Real

---

### 1. Deny the Body / Deny the Mechanism

Both texts issue the same warning in nearly identical language: do not deny the body or the mechanism. Spirituality does not require pretending the physical world does not exist.

Course in Miracles: "The body is merely a fact in human experience. Its abilities can be and frequently are over-evaluated. HOWEVER, IT IS ALMOST IMPOSSIBLE TO DENY ITS EXISTENCE. Those who do so are engaging in a particularly unworthy form of denial." (acim oe txt 2.III.56)

Urantia Book: "The grand universe is mechanism as well as organism, mechanical and living: a living mechanism activated by a Supreme Mind. BUT TO DENY THE MECHANISM OF THE FINITE CREATION IS TO DENY FACT AND TO DISREGARD REALITY." (UB 118:9.4)


---

### 2. Truth Is Changeless

Truth is not fragile. It cannot be damaged by our errors or improved by our efforts. It simply is.

Course in Miracles: "Truth is beyond your ability to destroy but entirely within your grasp. It belongs to you because you created it. It is yours because it is a part of you, just as you are part of God because He created you." (acim oe txt 5.VI.46)

Urantia Book: "Whatsoever God does, it shall be forever; nothing can be added to it nor anything taken from it." (UB 2:2.2)


---

### 3. Projection as Fundamental Law

Both texts use the same word for how God creates: projection. God creates by projecting outward. We, made in his likeness, create the same way.

Course in Miracles: "PROJECTION IS A FUNDAMENTAL LAW OF THE MIND and therefore one which *always* operates. It is the law by which you create and were created." (acim oe txt 7.IX.82)

Urantia Book: "With the Son and in the Spirit did God PROJECT eternal Havona, and ever since has there obtained the eternal pattern of co-ordinate participation in creation: SHARING." (UB 54:2.1)

Urantia Book: "Thus did the Father PROJECT reality in two actual phases." (UB 11:9.3)


---

### 4. Did the Universe Come from Mind?

Both texts reject pure materialism. The universe originates in mind, not matter. If mind made the universe, then mind can also transcend it.

Course in Miracles: "The Holy Spirit must perceive time and reinterpret it into the timeless. The mind must be led into eternity through time because, having made time, it is capable of perceiving its opposite." (acim oe txt 5.V.42)

Urantia Book: "Mechanisms do not absolutely dominate the total creation; the universe of universes in toto is mind planned, mind made, and mind administered." (UB 42:11.2)


---

### 5. What Is Real? Changelessness

Both texts define reality by the same criterion: changelessness. What is temporary is not ultimately real. What is eternal is. The foundation is what remains when everything temporary has passed.

Course in Miracles: "It cannot change with time or mood or chance. Its changelessness is what makes it real." (acim oe txt 14.V.39)

Urantia Book: "The eternal real is the good of the universe and not the time illusions of space evil." (UB 102:3.15)


---

## Time, Miracles, and Eternity

---

### 1. Past-Eternity Memory

The Course says remembering is restoring what is already there. The Urantia Book reveals where those memories come from: the Adjuster carries "past-eternity experience and memory" that becomes ours at fusion.

Course in Miracles: "To remember is merely to restore to your mind *what is already there*. You do not *make* what you remember; you merely accept again what has been made but was rejected." (acim oe txt 9.IX.70)

Urantia Book: "Thought Adjuster fusion imparts eternal actualities to personality which were previously only potential. Among these new endowments may be mentioned: fixation of divinity quality, PAST-ETERNITY EXPERIENCE AND MEMORY, immortality." (UB 112:7.1)


---

### 2. Timelessness and Miracles

The Course describes stepping "a moment into timelessness." The Urantia Book reveals the mechanics: for a Creator Son, any lapse of time consciousness would enact whatever he conceived, without the intervention of time. What one text offers as spiritual poetry, the other confirms as cosmic fact.

Course in Miracles: "What is the face of Christ but his who went a moment into timelessness and brought a clear reflection of the unity he felt an instant back to bless the world?" (acim oe wrk 169.12)

Urantia Book: "In order to prevent the appearance of apparent time miracles, it was necessary for Jesus to remain constantly time conscious. Any lapse of time consciousness on his part... was equivalent to the enactment of the thing conceived in the mind of this Creator Son, and without the intervention of time." (UB 136:5.5)


---

### 3. Language Limits When Describing Eternity

Human language breaks down when describing eternity. Both texts are remarkably self-aware about this limitation. They are using sequential language to describe something that has no sequence.

Course in Miracles: "God does not take steps because His accomplishments are not gradual. HE DOES NOTHING LAST BECAUSE HE CREATED FIRST AND FOR ALWAYS. IT MUST BE UNDERSTOOD THAT THE WORD 'FIRST' AS APPLIED TO HIM IS NOT A TIME CONCEPT." (acim oe txt 7.II.7)

Urantia Book: "WE SPEAK OF GOD'S 'FIRST' THOUGHT AND ALLUDE TO AN IMPOSSIBLE TIME ORIGIN of the Eternal Son for the purpose of gaining access to the thought channels of the human intellect. Such distortions of language represent our best efforts at CONTACT-COMPROMISE WITH THE TIME-BOUND MINDS OF MORTAL CREATURES." (UB 6:0.2)


---

### 4. Time-Space Belief and the Atonement

Neither text treats time-space as an accident. It was designed with a destination in mind. Both describe a universe that was built to bring its inhabitants home.

Course in Miracles: "The Atonement principle was in effect long before the Atonement itself began... ACTS WERE NOT NECESSARY BEFORE THE SEPARATION BECAUSE THE TIME-SPACE BELIEF DID NOT EXIST." (acim oe txt 2.II.36)

Course in Miracles: "The Atonement was BUILT INTO THE SPACE-TIME BELIEF IN ORDER TO SET A LIMIT ON THE NEED FOR THE BELIEF." (acim oe txt 2.II.39)

Urantia Book: "The same creative design which purposed evolution likewise provide those external RESTRAINTS OF TIME AND SPACE, HUNGER AND FEAR, WHICH EFFECTIVELY CIRCUMSCRIBE THE SUBSPIRITUAL CHOICE RANGE of such uncultured creatures." (UB 118:8.5)


---

### 5. Spirit Energy Can Be Manipulated

Spirit is not vague or unpredictable. It follows principles and produces consistent, observable results. You know it by what it does.

Course in Miracles: "YET IF YOU SEE ITS EFFECTS, YOU KNOW IT MUST BE THERE. By perceiving what it *does*, you recognize its being." (acim oe txt 11.VIII.62)

Course in Miracles: "The Holy Spirit is invisible, but you can see the *results* of His Presence, and through them you will learn that He is there." (acim oe txt 11.VIII.63)

Urantia Book: "Spiritual energy acts in accordance with established laws, just as does physical energy. There are just as certain and reliable laws in the spirit world as obtain in the material realms." (UB 44:5.4)

## The Separation and the Rebellion

---

### 1. Angels Withdrawn After Adamic Default

Both texts describe a catastrophic event on our world that resulted in the withdrawal of celestial assistance. The Course tells it as a story. The Urantia Book records it as history.

Course in Miracles: "Many Souls offered their efforts on behalf of the separated ones, but they could not withstand the strength of the attack and had to be brought back. Angels came, too, but their protection did not suffice." (acim oe txt 2.II.38)

Urantia Book: "On Urantia the majority of the planetary helpers were removed upon the collapse of the Adamic regime, and the seraphic supervision of your world devolved to a greater extent upon the administrators." (UB 39:5.2)


---

### 2. World of Unreality from the Rebellion

The Course speaks in abstract terms about the catastrophe. The Urantia Book names it, dates it, and describes its consequences. Both agree: our world has been fundamentally distorted by an event that was never supposed to happen.

Course in Miracles: "You have expressed surprise at hearing how very different is reality from what you see. You do not realize the magnitude of that one error. It was so vast and so completely incredible that from it a world of total unreality had to emerge. What else could come of it? Its fragmented aspects are fearful enough, as you begin to look at them. But nothing you have seen begins to show you the enormity of the original error, which seemed to cast you out of Heaven, to shatter knowledge into meaningless bits of disunited perceptions, and to force you to further substitutions." (acim oe txt 18.II.5)

Urantia Book: "The problems associated with human existence on Urantia are impossible of understanding without a knowledge of certain great epochs of the past, notably the occurrence and consequences of the planetary rebellion... IT DID MARKEDLY MODIFY THE COURSE OF SOCIAL EVOLUTION AND OF SPIRITUAL DEVELOPMENT." (UB 67:0.1)


---

### 3. The Separation Theme

Both texts place responsibility on the created, not the Creator. The Father did not withdraw. We withdrew. And both treat the separation as temporary.

Course in Miracles: "Until the 'separation,' which is a better term than the 'fall,' nothing was lacking. This meant that man had no needs at all." (acim oe txt 1.I.90)

Urantia Book: "The Father is not in spiritual hiding, but so many of his creatures have hidden themselves away in the mists of their own willful decisions AND FOR THE TIME BEING HAVE SEPARATED THEMSELVES from the communion of his spirit." (UB 5:1.10)

## Spiritual Practice

---

### 1. Words Are Valueless: Spirit Content Matters

Prayer is not about getting the words right. It is about the sincerity of the heart. Both texts say so in nearly identical terms.

Course in Miracles: "Strictly speaking, words play no part at all in healing. The motivating factor is prayer, or asking... Words are but symbols of symbols. They are thus twice removed from reality." (acim oe mnl 21.1)

Urantia Book: "It is the motivating thought, the spiritual content, that validates the mortal supplication. Words are valueless." (UB 7:3.7)


---

### 2. Reliable but Wrong / Right in Fact, Wrong in Truth

Two sentences that say the same thing in almost the same words. Facts are not truth. You can master the one and completely miss the other.

Course in Miracles: "You can be wholly reliable and *entirely* wrong." (acim oe txt 1.II.103)

Urantia Book: "One can be technically right as to fact and everlastingly wrong in the truth." (UB 48:6.33)


---

### 3. Listen, Learn, and Do / Action Required

Both texts reject passive spirituality. Understanding is not the goal. Understanding that leads to action is.

Course in Miracles: "The slogan for the Crusade is 'Listen, learn and *do*'... THE FIRST TWO ARE NOT ENOUGH. THE REAL MEMBERS OF MY PARTY ARE ACTIVE WORKERS." (acim oe txt 1.I.31)

Urantia Book: "WITH GOD THE FATHER, sonship is the great relationship. With God the Supreme, achievement is the prerequisite to status: ONE MUST DO SOMETHING AS WELL AS BE SOMETHING." (UB 115:0.1)

Urantia Book: "Mankind does not ascend effortlessly in the universe, neither does the Supreme evolve without purposeful and intelligent action." (UB 117:4.7)


---

### 4. Spiritual Experiences Are for Believers

Miracles are not arguments. They are not demonstrations for skeptics. They are shared between those who already know, to strengthen what they know.

Course in Miracles: "The use of miracles as spectacles to induce belief is wrong, or better, is a misunderstanding of their purpose. They are really used for and by believers." (acim oe txt 1.I.10)

Urantia Book: "The God-knowing man describes his spiritual experiences, not to convince unbelievers, but for the edification and mutual satisfaction of believers." (UB 1:6.6)


---

### 5. Elect to Stay Behind After Fusion

Both texts describe beings who have reached the threshold of release but choose to stay for the sake of others. The Course states the principle. The Urantia Book reveals that it actually happens.

Course in Miracles: "THE ONLY VALUE WHICH THE BODY HAS IS TO ENABLE YOU TO BRING YOUR BROTHERS TO THE BRIDGE WITH YOU AND TO BE RELEASED TOGETHER THERE." (acim oe txt 16.VII.63)

Urantia Book: "Mortals of Adjuster-fusion status who, UPON THEIR OWN PETITION, ARE TEMPORARILY EXEMPTED FROM TRANSLATION by the order of the Personalized Adjuster of universe chieftainship in order that they may continue on the planet in certain important administrative posts." (UB 55:3.18)


---

### 6. Peace and Understanding Go Together

Peace and understanding are inseparable. If your mind is agitated, something in your understanding is wrong. This is a diagnostic tool, not a sentimental idea.

Course in Miracles: "Think not you understand anything until you pass the test of perfect peace, for peace and understanding go together and never can be found alone." (acim oe txt 14.VII.72)

Urantia Book: "And there can be no peace in the heart or progress in the mind unless you fall wholeheartedly in love with truth." (UB 157:2.2)


---

### 7. Practice and Commotion

Neither text promises a smooth ride. The commotion is not a sign that something is wrong. It is a sign that something is growing.

Course in Miracles: "The speed by which it can be reached depends on this one thing alone: your willingness to practice every step." (acim oe txt 30.I.1)

Urantia Book: "Religious perplexities are inevitable; there can be no growth without psychic conflict and spiritual agitation. The organization of a philosophic standard of living entails considerable commotion." (UB 100:4.2)


---

### 8. Give and Receive Are One

True giving is not sacrifice. It is increase. What you give away, you gain. The entire message of both books comes down to this.

Course in Miracles: "A major learning goal this course has set is to reverse your view of giving, so you can receive. For giving has become a source of fear... God's gifts will never lessen when they are given away. They but increase thereby." (acim oe wrk 105.3)

Urantia Book: "The Master was always generous. He never grew weary of saying, 'It is more blessed to give than to receive.' Said he, 'Freely you have received, freely give.'" (UB 100:7.10)
