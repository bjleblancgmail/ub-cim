## Section 1: What Is a Miracle?

The word "miracle" has been cheapened. We use it for parking spaces and sports victories. We use it for anything surprising. But both the Urantia Book and the Course in Miracles treat miracles with precision, and their definitions reveal something profound about the nature of reality.

The Course opens with fifty miracle principles. The very first:

> "There is no order of difficulty among miracles. One is not 'harder' or 'bigger' than another. They are all the same." (acim oe txt 1.I.1)

This baffles the material mind. Surely curing cancer is harder than curing a headache? Surely raising the dead is bigger than healing a cold? But the Course insists: no. To the eternal perspective, there is no difference. Why?

Because miracles do not operate by material laws. They operate by something else entirely.

The Urantia Book provides the framework:

> "It therefore becomes evident that any possible supernatural or supposedly superhuman accompaniments of Jesus' ministry pertained wholly to the elimination of time unless the Father in heaven specifically ruled otherwise." (UB 136:5.5)

The elimination of time. This is the key. Miracles are not violations of natural law. They are operations that bypass the time factor. What would take years happens in moments. What would take generations happens now. The laws of nature are not broken; they are accelerated beyond recognition.

This is why there is no order of difficulty. From the eternal perspective, where time does not constrain, healing a headache and healing cancer require the same thing: the removal of the temporal delay between cause and effect. In eternity, all healing is instantaneous. Miracles simply allow that eternity to touch time.

And the Course names the force that makes this possible:

> "The Holy Spirit is the motivation for miracle-mindedness. It is the will to heal the separation by letting it go. The miracle itself is just this fusion or union of will between Father and Son." (acim oe txt 5.II.9)

The miracle itself is just this fusion of will. The Course uses the word. When the human will and the divine will unite, miracles happen. The Urantia Book confirms this with a law the celestial authors say they have long known:

> "The explanation, then, of many of these cases of healing must be found in a great law which has long been known to us, namely, What the Creator Son desires and the eternal Father wills IS." (UB 149:1.7)

What the Creator Son desires and the Father wills IS. Not "will be." Not "may be." IS. When divine will and human faith align, reality reorganizes itself. The temporal delay between desire and manifestation simply disappears.

Two books. One mechanism. Fusion of will.

## Section 2: Miracles Abolish Time

Both books state this explicitly, in remarkably similar language.

The Course says:

> "The miracle thus has the unique property of shortening time by rendering the space of time it occupies unnecessary." (acim oe txt 1.I.83)

And the Course states it more directly:

> "A miracle is a beginning and an ending. It thus abolishes time. It is always an affirmation of rebirth, which seems to go back, but really goes forward. It undoes the past in the present, and thus releases the future." (acim oe txt 1.I.13)

Undoes the past in the present, and releases the future. The miracle does not merely speed things up. It reorganizes the relationship between past, present, and future entirely.

The Course describes the process:

> "The miracle abolishes time. It does this by a process of collapsing it. It thus abolishes certain intervals within it. It does this, however, within the larger temporal sequence." (acim oe txt 1.I.84)

The Course explains what this means for the one who receives it:

> "The miracle is a learning device which lessens the need for time." (acim oe txt 1.I.82)

And the Course says:

> "It substitutes for learning that might have taken thousands of years." (acim oe txt 1.I.83)

How? Not by skipping the learning. Not by granting knowledge you have not earned. But by removing the obstacle that made the learning take so long. When you recognize another person as your equal in the Sonship, all the ego-driven lessons that separation would have forced you to learn simply fall away.

The Urantia Book uses nearly identical language from the cosmic side. Speaking of Jesus' healings:

> "Even when he permitted the manifestation of numerous time-shortening ministrations of mercy, he almost invariably admonished the recipients of his healing ministry to tell no man about the benefits they had received." (UB 136:8.1)

Time-shortening ministrations of mercy. That is the Urantia Book's definition of a miracle. And when the Urantia Book describes the sundown healing at Capernaum, where hundreds were healed at once, it reaches for extraordinary language:

> "The majority of those who were recipients of supernatural or creative physical healing at this sundown demonstration of divine energy were not permanently spiritually benefited by this extraordinary manifestation of mercy. The spiritual kingdom was not advanced in the hearts of men by this amazing eruption of timeless creative healing." (UB 145:3.14)

An eruption of timeless creative healing. Time did not merely shorten. It erupted into timelessness. And yet the Urantia Book notes a sobering truth: physical healing without spiritual transformation is incomplete. The spiritual kingdom was not advanced. The bodies were healed, but the hearts were unchanged.

The Course teaches the same. A miracle is not primarily about the body. It is about perception:

> "A miracle rearranges the order of perception, and places the levels in their true perspective. This heals at all levels, because all sickness comes from confusing the levels." (acim oe txt 1.I.66)

Sickness comes from confusing the levels. When you mistake the body for the self, when you confuse the temporal for the eternal, you are sick at the root. The miracle corrects that confusion. It places levels in their true perspective: spirit above mind, mind above matter. And when that order is restored, healing follows naturally, because what was out of alignment snaps back into place.

## Section 3: The Mechanism of Healing

How did the healings actually happen?

The Urantia Book offers the most systematic explanation found in either text. The celestial authors identify three conditions that had to coexist:

> "We believe that many of these apparent miracles of healing, as they occurred in the course of Jesus' earth ministry, were the result of the coexistence of the following three powerful, potent, and associated influences: 1. The presence of strong, dominant, and living faith in the heart of the human being who persistently sought healing, together with the fact that such healing was desired for its spiritual benefits rather than for purely physical restoration." (UB 149:1.4-5)

The first condition: living faith. Not passive belief. Not hoping for the best. A faith so strong it became a creative force. The Urantia Book continues:

> "It is, then, our opinion that, in the personal presence of Jesus, certain forms of profound human faith were literally and truly compelling in the manifestation of healing by certain creative forces and personalities of the universe who were at that time so intimately associated with the Son of Man. It therefore becomes a fact of record that Jesus did frequently suffer men to heal themselves in his presence by their powerful, personal faith." (UB 149:1.8)

People healed themselves. Their faith did not ask permission. It compelled. Jesus did not consciously will every healing. The creative forces responded to faith itself, in the presence of the Creator Son.

Consider the woman the apostles called Veronica. For years she had been afflicted with a hemorrhage. She had spent everything on physicians. None could cure her. Then she heard of Jesus, pressed through the crowd, and touched the border of his garment. In that moment, she was healed. Years of suffering, collapsed into a single instant. The Urantia Book says:

> "Her faith was of the sort that laid direct hold upon the creative power resident in the Master's person. With the faith she had, it was only necessary to approach the Master's person. It was not at all necessary to touch his garment; that was merely the superstitious part of her belief." (UB 152:0.3)

But Jesus would not let her leave with the wrong understanding. The Urantia Book records:

> "He did not want Veronica to go away thinking that her tempting to steal her cure had been honored, or that her superstition in associating the touch of his garment with her healing had been effective. He desired all to know that it was her pure and living faith that had wrought the cure." (UB 152:0.3)

It was not the cloth. It was recognition. In one moment of pure faith, the temporal process that had dragged on for years simply became unnecessary.

The Course describes the same mechanism from the inner side. What faith does in the presence of Jesus, forgiveness does in every encounter:

> "The miracle enables you to see your brother without his past, and so perceive him as born again. His errors are all past, and by perceiving him without them, you are releasing him. And since his past is yours, you share in this release." (acim oe txt 12.IV.5)

To see your brother without his past is to step outside of time. In that moment, you are not looking at history. You are looking at reality. And in that recognition, both of you are released. The Course says:

> "The miracle, as an expression of true human charity, can only shorten time at most. It must be understood, however, that whenever a man offers a miracle to another, he is shortening the suffering of *both*." (acim oe txt 2.III.71)

Both. Not just the one who is healed. The one who heals is also shortened. The doer and the receiver emerge farther along in time than they would otherwise have been.

Many of the healings at the Bethsaida hospital worked this way. The Urantia Book explains:

> "Many of the cures effected by Jesus in connection with his ministry in behalf of Elman's patients did, indeed, appear to resemble the working of miracles, but we were instructed that they were only just such transformations of mind and spirit as may occur in the experience of expectant and faith-dominated persons who are under the immediate and inspirational influence of a strong, positive, and beneficent personality whose ministry banishes fear and destroys anxiety." (UB 148:2.2)

Transformations of mind and spirit. A personality whose ministry banishes fear and destroys anxiety. This is what both books are saying: the miracle is not magic. It is the natural result of fear being replaced by love, of confusion being replaced by clarity, of the temporal being touched by the eternal. When fear goes, what it was holding in place goes with it.

## Section 4: Jesus and Time Consciousness

The Urantia Book offers a fascinating detail about Jesus that illuminates the entire subject.

During the forty days in the wilderness, after his baptism, Jesus received instructions from his Personalized Adjuster about the use of his divine powers. The Adjuster explained that while the celestial hosts could be limited in space by Jesus's authority, they could not be limited in time:

> "In accepting this command of the universe hosts in attendance upon Christ Michael, the Personalized Adjuster took great pains to point out to Jesus that, while such an assembly of universe creatures could be limited in their space activities by the delegated authority of their Creator, such limitations were not operative in connection with their function in time." (UB 136:5.4)

Time was the variable. And the Urantia Book describes the staggering consequence:

> "In order to prevent the appearance of apparent time miracles, it was necessary for Jesus to remain constantly time conscious. Any lapse of time consciousness on his part, in connection with the entertainment of definite desire, was equivalent to the enactment of the thing conceived in the mind of this Creator Son, and without the intervention of time." (UB 136:5.5)

If Jesus conceived a desire and momentarily forgot about time, even for an instant, the thing would simply happen. Immediately. Without delay. His mind, connected to the creative power of a Creator Son, could manifest reality the moment time-awareness lapsed.

This is why Jesus performed relatively few miracles compared to what he could have done. He had to stay constantly time conscious, deliberately keeping himself anchored in the temporal flow so that his every passing thought did not immediately materialize.

The one time he deliberately chose otherwise was the feeding of the five thousand. The Urantia Book says:

> "In this case, so we were taught, Michael multiplied food elements as he always does except for the elimination of the time factor and the visible life channel." (UB 152:2.10)

As he always does. The food did not appear from nothing. It grew, as all food grows, through the life processes that God designed. But the time factor was removed. What takes a season happened in a moment. Natural law was not broken. It was fulfilled, instantly.

The Course hints at the same power available to all minds aligned with God:

> "What you need to learn now is that only infinite patience can produce immediate effects. This is the way in which time is exchanged for eternity. Infinite patience calls upon infinite Love, and by producing results now renders time unnecessary." (acim oe txt 5.VI.15)

Only infinite patience can produce immediate effects. The paradox is precise. You cannot rush eternity. But when you stop rushing, when patience becomes infinite, when you release the demand that things happen on your schedule, you open the door for eternity to act. And eternity acts immediately. Time is exchanged for eternity. Results appear now.

Jesus demonstrated what this looks like when fully realized. The rest of us practice it in smaller ways, with every act of patience, every release of control, every moment we trust God's timing over our own.

## Section 5: The Laws of Eternity

The Course states the principle directly:

> "Miracles therefore rest on the laws of eternity, not of time." (acim oe txt 1.I.19)

Material law operates within time. Cause precedes effect. Seeds grow into trees through seasons. Wounds heal through cellular processes that take days or weeks. These are the laws of time, and they are real. But they are not the only laws.

Eternal law operates outside time. In eternity, cause and effect are simultaneous. There is no waiting, no sequence, no delay. What is true is simply true: immediately, completely, forever.

The Course describes the scope of what happens when eternal law touches temporal existence:

> "What the Holy Spirit enables you to do is clearly not of this world, for miracles violate every law of reality, as this world judges it. Every law of time and space, of magnitude and mass, of prediction and control, is transcended." (acim oe txt 11.VI.3)

Every law of time and space, transcended. Not some laws. Every law. But the Urantia Book explains why this is possible. It is not chaos. It is a deeper order:

> "The spirit is the creative reality; the physical counterpart is the time-space reflection of the spirit reality, the physical repercussion of the creative action of spirit-mind." (UB 42:12.14)

Physical reality is the reflection. Spirit is the original. What we see in time and space is the repercussion of what happens first in spirit. The Urantia Book puts it even more plainly:

> "On a material world you think of a body as having a spirit, but we regard the spirit as having a body. The material eyes are truly the windows of the spirit-born soul. The spirit is the architect, the mind is the builder, the body is the material building." (UB 42:12.12)

The spirit is the architect. The mind is the builder. The body is the material building. When the architect changes the blueprint, the building changes. When spirit acts through mind, matter responds. This is not magic. This is the actual order of reality, inverted from what the material mind assumes.

And the Urantia Book confirms the hierarchy holds:

> "Mind universally dominates matter, even as it is in turn responsive to the ultimate overcontrol of spirit." (UB 42:12.15)

Spirit directs mind. Mind dominates matter. When a miracle occurs, this chain of command simply becomes visible. What is always true becomes momentarily apparent. The laws of eternity briefly override the laws of time, and we call it a miracle.

We sense this without understanding it. The Urantia Book says:

> "It is only natural that mortal man should be harassed by feelings of insecurity as he views himself inextricably bound to nature while he possesses spiritual powers wholly transcendent to all things temporal and finite." (UB 111:6.8)

You feel bound to nature. You also possess powers that transcend it. The tension between these two facts is the human condition. Miracles are the moments when the transcendent breaks through.

The Course frames this as an invitation:

> "Since only eternity is real, why not use the illusion of time constructively?" (acim oe txt 1.I.71)

And the Urantia Book reveals that the time delay itself is not punishment but provision:

> "This superuniverse time lag, this obstacle to perfection attainment, provides for creature participation in evolutionary growth. It thus makes it possible for the creature to enter into partnership with the Creator in the evolution of that selfsame creature." (UB 106:1.2)

Time gives you room to participate. Without it, everything would happen at once and you would have no role. The time lag is not an obstacle to growth. It is the space in which growth occurs. It is the scaffolding that lets you build alongside God.

But miracles remind us that the scaffolding is temporary. They are windows into what lies behind time, promises that the slow world we inhabit is surrounded by a fast world we cannot see. And one day, when the scaffolding comes down, we will live there.

## Section 6: What Miracles Require

If miracles are possible, why don't they happen constantly?

The Course answers: they do. We simply do not recognize them.

> "Miracles are habits and should be involuntary. They should not be under conscious control. Consciously selected miracles can be misguided." (acim oe txt 1.I.5)

The Course also says:

> "Miracles occur naturally as expressions of love. The real miracle is the love that inspires them. In this sense, everything that comes from love is a miracle." (acim oe txt 1.I.3)

Miracles are habits because love is natural. They are not extraordinary feats. They are what happens when fear steps aside and love acts unobstructed.

Every time you choose peace instead of conflict, time shortens. Every time you forgive instead of resent, a wall comes down. Every time you see someone as a child of God rather than as their mistakes, you clear the path for both of you. These are miracles. They may not look dramatic. They are still eliminations of time.

The Course says miracles belong to a larger pattern:

> "Miracles are a part of an interlocking chain of forgiveness which, when completed, is the Atonement. This process works all the time and in all dimensions of time." (acim oe txt 1.I.69)

An interlocking chain. Each miracle connects to the next. Each forgiveness enables the next. And the whole chain, when complete, is the Atonement itself, the full correction of the separation. The Course describes how this process accelerates:

> "If a sufficient number of people become truly miracle-minded quickly, the shortening process can be almost immeasurable. But it is essential that these individuals free themselves from fear sooner than would ordinarily be the case, because they must emerge from basic conflict if they are to bring peace to the minds of others." (acim oe txt 2.IV.1)

A sufficient number, freed from fear, can shorten the process almost immeasurably. This is not theory. This is both books describing the same possibility: that awakened minds accelerate the awakening of all minds.

The Urantia Book names the mechanism that connects individual prayer to collective transformation:

> "Prayer is the sincere and longing look of the child to its spirit Father; it is a psychologic process of exchanging the human will for the divine will. Prayer is a part of the divine plan for making over that which is into that which ought to be." (UB 144:4.9)

Making over that which is into that which ought to be. That is a definition of miracle. And the tool is prayer, the exchange of human will for divine will. The same fusion of will that the Course describes as the miracle itself.

And though Jesus is no longer physically present, his power has not diminished. The Urantia Book says:

> "Though the Master is absent as a material being, he is present as a spiritual influence in the hearts of men. By going away from the world, the Master made it possible for his spirit to live alongside that of his Father which indwells the minds of all mankind." (UB 152:1.5)

His spirit lives alongside the Adjuster in every mind. The same creative power that healed Veronica, that fed the five thousand, that erupted in timeless healing at Capernaum, is present now. Not in the same physical way. But the Course reveals where all of this leads:

> "The miracle-worker, therefore, accepts the time-control factor gladly because he recognizes that every collapse of time brings all men closer to the ultimate release from time in which the Son and the Father are one." (acim oe txt 1.I.87)

Every miracle you perform or receive is not just a personal healing. It is a step toward the moment when time ends and Father and Son are one. This is the fusion thesis at work inside the miracle principles: every collapse of time is a step toward fusion.

Miracles require love, and love requires union. The isolated ego cannot perform miracles because it is cut off from the source of creative power. But the mind joined with God, aware of its connection to all other minds, that mind participates in eternity. And eternity is where miracles come from.
