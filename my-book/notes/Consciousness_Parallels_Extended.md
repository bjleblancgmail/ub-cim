# Consciousness in A Course in Miracles and The Urantia Book
## Extended Parallel Quotations and Validation

---

## I. THE THREE-TIERED MODEL OF MIND

### A. The Architecture Stated Explicitly

**ACIM (T 1B 24e):**
> "Miracles come from the (below conscious) (subconscious) level. Revelations come from the above conscious level. The conscious level is in between & reacts to either sub- or super-conscious impulses in varying ratios. Freud was right about the classification, but not the names."

**ACIM (T 3 F 3):**
> "In our picture of the psyche, there is an unconscious level, which properly consists ONLY of the miracle ability and should be under MY direction; and a conscious level, which perceives or is aware of impulses from both the unconscious and the superconscious. These are the sources of the impulses it receives. Consciousness is thus the level of perception, but NOT of knowledge."

**Urantia Book (100:5.6):**
> "If one is disposed to recognize a theoretical subconscious mind as a practical working hypothesis in the otherwise unified intellectual life, then, to be consistent, one should postulate a similar and corresponding realm of ascending intellectual activity as the superconscious level, the zone of immediate contact with the indwelling spirit entity, the Thought Adjuster."

**Urantia Book (110:4.3):**
> "In contrast with these subconscious emanations, THE REVELATIONS OF THE ADJUSTER APPEAR THROUGH THE REALMS OF THE SUPERCONSCIOUS."

**Convergence:** Both texts present an identical three-tiered model: subconscious (below), conscious (middle), superconscious (above). Both identify the superconscious as the zone of divine contact. Both distinguish subconscious emanations from superconscious revelations.

---

### B. Consciousness Rests Between Two Systems

**ACIM (Use of Terms, U 2 A 6):**
> "CONSCIOUSNESS is the receptive mechanism, receiving messages from above or below; from the Holy Spirit or the ego. Consciousness has levels and awareness can shift quite dramatically, but it cannot transcend the perceptual realm. At its highest it becomes aware of the real world, and can be trained to do so increasingly. Yet the very fact that it has levels and can be trained demonstrates that consciousness cannot reach knowledge."

**Urantia Book (111:1.5):**
> "Human consciousness rests gently upon the electrochemical mechanism below and delicately touches the spirit-morontia energy system above. Of neither of these two systems is the human being ever completely conscious in his mortal life; therefore must he work in mind, of which he is conscious."

**Convergence:** Both describe consciousness as positioned between two systems — one material (below), one spiritual (above). Both say consciousness receives from both directions. Both say consciousness cannot fully access the higher system while mortal. ACIM says it "cannot transcend the perceptual realm"; UB says the human is "never completely conscious" of either system.

---

## II. CONSCIOUSNESS DOES NOT CREATE — IT CHOOSES

**ACIM (T 1B 24k):**
> "Neither [miracles nor revelations] emanates from consciousness, but both are EXPERIENCED there. This is essential, because consciousness is the state which PRODUCES action, though it DOES NOT Inspire it."

**ACIM (T 1B 24e):**
> "Consciousness is the level which engages in the world, and is capable of responding to both external & internal impulses. Having no impulses from itself, and being primarily a mechanism for inducing response, it can be very wrong."

**Urantia Book (196:3.10):**
> "The human mind does not create real values; human experience does not yield universe insight. Concerning insight, the recognition of moral values and the discernment of spiritual meanings, all that the human mind can do is to discover, recognize, interpret, and choose."

**Urantia Book (111:4.2):**
> "Meanings are nonexistent in a wholly sensory or material world. Meanings and values are only perceived in the inner or supermaterial spheres of human experience."

**Convergence:** Both texts agree that consciousness is not generative — it is a switchboard, not a power plant. ACIM: it has "no impulses from itself." UB: all it can do is "discover, recognize, interpret, and choose." The real creative impulses come from above (superconscious/Adjuster) and below (subconscious/miracle-level).

---

## III. CONSCIOUSNESS IS THE DOMAIN OF THE EGO

**ACIM (T 3 F 4-5):**
> "Consciousness was the first split that man introduced into himself. He became a PERCEIVER rather than a creator in the true sense. Consciousness is correctly identified as the domain of the ego."

**ACIM (T 5 E 8):**
> "Time is a belief of the ego, so the lower mind, which IS the ego's domain, accepts it without question."

**Urantia Book (16:9.4):**
> "Human self-consciousness implies the recognition of the reality of selves other than the conscious self and further implies that such awareness is mutual; that the self is known as it knows."

**Convergence:** ACIM explicitly identifies consciousness as "the domain of the ego" — the level where perception, selectivity, and judgment operate. The UB treats self-consciousness similarly as the realm of perceiving selves, distinct from the God-consciousness that operates at the superconscious level. Both distinguish the ego-driven conscious self from the deeper divine knowing (Soul/Adjuster).

---

## IV. THE EGO INVERTS SUPERCONSCIOUS IMPULSES

**ACIM (T 4 D 5-6):**
> "In its characteristic upside down way, the ego has taken the impulses from the superconscious and perceives them as if they arise in the unconscious. The ego judges what is to be accepted, and the impulses from the superconscious are unacceptable to it, because they clearly point to the unexistence of the ego itself. The ego therefore experiences threat, and not only censors but also re-interprets the data."
>
> "Repression thus operates to conceal not only the baser impulses, but also the most lofty ones from the ego's awareness, because BOTH are threatening to the ego... The threat value of the lofty is really much greater to the ego, because the pull of God Himself can hardly be equated with the pull of human appetites."

**ACIM (T 4 D 7):**
> "The upper level of the unconscious thus contains the call of God as well as the call of the body. That is why the basic conflict between love and fear is unconscious. The ego cannot tolerate either and represses both by resorting to inhibition. Society depends on inhibiting the former, but SALVATION depends on disinhibiting the latter."

**Urantia Book (100:5.9):**
> "The characteristics of the mystical state are diffusion of consciousness with vivid islands of focal attention operating on a comparatively passive intellect. All of this gravitates consciousness toward the subconscious rather than in the direction of the zone of spiritual contact, the superconscious. Many mystics have carried their mental dissociation to the level of abnormal mental manifestations."

**Urantia Book (108:5.8):**
> "The Thought Adjusters would like to change your feelings of fear to convictions of love and confidence; but they cannot mechanically and arbitrarily do such things; that is your task. In executing those decisions which deliver you from the fetters of fear, you literally supply the psychic fulcrum on which the Adjuster may subsequently apply a spiritual lever of uplifting and advancing illumination."

**Convergence:** Both texts describe the same distortion mechanism. The ego/material mind gravitates consciousness *downward* toward the subconscious rather than *upward* toward the superconscious divine contact zone. Both say fear blocks the divine connection. ACIM says the ego "censors and reinterprets" superconscious data. UB says consciousness gravitates toward the subconscious rather than toward "the zone of spiritual contact." Both insist the mortal must make choices to remove fear so the divine partner can function.

## V. THE UNWATCHED MIND FILLS THE SUBCONSCIOUS WITH FEAR

**ACIM (T 2 E 21-23):**
> "You persist in believing that when you do not consciously watch your mind, it is unmindful. It is time to consider the whole world of the unconscious, or unwatched mind. This will frighten you, because it is the source of fright."
>
> "The unwatched mind is responsible for the whole content of the unconscious, which lies above the miracle-level."

**ACIM (T 1B 37o):**
> "Both of you are involved with unconscious distortions (above the miracle level), which are producing a dense cover over miracle-impulses which makes it hard for them to reach consciousness."

**ACIM (T 1B 24m):**
> "The deeper levels of his subconscious always contain the impulse to Miracles, but he is free to fill its superficial levels, which are closer to consciousness, with the impulses of this world and to identify himself with them. This results in denying himself access to the miracle level underneath."

**Urantia Book (177:4.11) — The Case of Judas:**
> "Judas did not realize it at this time, but he had been a subconscious critic of Jesus ever since John the Baptist was beheaded by Herod... And all these accumulations of human resentment and bitter disappointment which Judas had LAID BY IN HIS SOUL IN HABILIMENTS OF HATE WERE NOW WELL ORGANIZED IN HIS SUBCONSCIOUS MIND and ready to spring up to engulf him."

**Urantia Book (108:6.2):**
> "The indwelling Adjusters are particularly tormented by those thoughts which are purely sordid and selfish; they are distressed by irreverence for that which is beautiful and divine, and they are virtually thwarted in their work by many of man's foolish animal fears and childish anxieties."

**Convergence:** Both describe the identical mechanism: accumulated fear, resentment, and worldly content fills the superficial subconscious, creating a barrier that blocks divine impulses from reaching consciousness. ACIM calls this the "dense cover over miracle-impulses." The UB illustrates it concretely through Judas, whose subconscious bitterness overwhelmed the Adjuster's influence. Both say animal fears and selfish thoughts actively thwart the divine partner's work.

## VI. SUPERCONSCIOUS BREAKTHROUGHS — FROM BOTH DIRECTIONS

**ACIM (T 1B 30s-t):**
> "Some slips reach consciousness from the un-Christ-controlled subconscious, and betray a lack of love. But others come from the superconscious, which IS in communion with God, and which can also break into consciousness."

**Urantia Book (110:4.3):**
> "Certain abrupt presentations of thoughts, conclusions, and other pictures of mind are sometimes the direct or indirect work of the Adjuster; but far more often they are the sudden emergence into consciousness of ideas which have been grouping themselves together in the submerged mental levels, natural and everyday occurrences of normal and ordinary psychic function inherent in the circuits of the evolving animal mind."

**Urantia Book (19:5.9):**
> "There is a vast body of essential spiritual knowledge, truth indispensable to high spiritual attainment, which cannot be consciously received; self-consciousness would effectively jeopardize the certainty of reception."

**ACIM (T 1B 25e):**
> "Revelation is intensely personal, and is actually not translatable into conscious content at all. That is why any attempt to describe it in words is usually incomprehensible."

**Convergence:** Both texts recognize that content breaks through into consciousness from both directions and stress the difficulty of distinguishing sources. Both also agree that the highest spiritual communications *cannot be fully translated into conscious content* — ACIM says revelation is "not translatable into conscious content at all"; UB says essential spiritual knowledge "cannot be consciously received."

## VII. THE SUPERCONSCIOUS KNOWS — IT CANNOT BE DARKENED

**ACIM (T 3 F 15):**
> "The mind returns itself to its proper function only when it WILLS TO KNOW. This places it in the Soul's service, where perception is meaningless. The superconscious is the level of the mind which wills to do this."

**ACIM (T 3 F 18):**
> "The superconscious, which KNOWS, could not be reconciled with this loss of power, because it was incapable of darkness. This is why it became almost inaccessible to the mind, and entirely inaccessible to the body."

**Urantia Book (100:1.9):**
> "The unconscious nature of religious growth does not, however, signify that it is an activity functioning in the supposed subconscious realms of human intellect; rather does it signify creative activities in the superconscious levels of mortal mind. The experience of the realization of the reality of unconscious religious growth is the one positive proof of the functional existence of the superconsciousness."

**Urantia Book (111:4.5):**
> "Only in the higher levels of the superconscious mind as it impinges upon the spirit realm of human experience can you find those higher concepts in association with effective master patterns which will contribute to the building of a better and more enduring civilization."

**Convergence:** Both texts identify the superconscious as the level that *knows* rather than *perceives*. Both say it is incapable of error or darkness. ACIM says it "KNOWS" and is "incapable of darkness." UB says it is where "creative activities" function and where "higher concepts in association with effective master patterns" are found. In both, it represents the divine level that persists regardless of what the ego/conscious mind is doing.

## VIII. JESUS'S ROLE: SEPARATING TRUE FROM FALSE IN THE UNCONSCIOUS

**ACIM (T 4 D 8):**
> "The reason you need my help is because you have repressed your own guide, and therefore need guidance. My role is to separate the true from the false in your own unconscious, so it can break through the barriers the ego has set up and shine into your minds. Against our united strength, the ego CANNOT prevail."

**Urantia Book (101:1.3):**
> "The divine spirit makes contact with mortal man, not by feelings or emotions, but in the realm of the highest and most spiritualized thinking. It is your thoughts, not your feelings, that lead you Godward. But the mind that really discerns God, hears the indwelling Adjuster, is the pure mind."

**Urantia Book (109:5.1):**
> "Supreme and self-acting Adjusters are often able to contribute factors of spiritual import to the human mind when it flows freely in the liberated but controlled channels of creative imagination. At such times... the Adjuster is able to arrest the mental currents, to stay the flow, and then to divert the idea procession; and all this is done in order to effect deep spiritual transformations in the higher recesses of the superconsciousness."

**Convergence:** In ACIM, Jesus describes his role as actively working within the unconscious mind to separate truth from ego-distortion, allowing the light to "break through the barriers." In the UB, the Adjuster performs the same function — working in the "higher recesses of the superconsciousness" to "divert the idea procession" and "effect deep spiritual transformations." Both describe a divine partner working within the mind structure to overcome the ego's barriers.

## IX. PRAYER BRIDGES THE GAP TO THE SUPERCONSCIOUS

**ACIM (Manual for Teachers, M 21:1):**
> "The motivating factor is prayer, or asking. What you ask for, you receive. But this refers to the prayer of the heart, not to the words you use in praying... words are but symbols of symbols. They are thus twice removed from reality."

**Urantia Book (91:2.6):**
> "There is also a domain of prayer wherein the intellectually alert and spiritually progressing individual attains more or less contact with the superconscious levels of the human mind, the domain of the indwelling Thought Adjuster."

**Urantia Book (91:3.5):**
> "Prayer induces the human ego to look both ways for help: for material aid to the subconscious reservoir of mortal experience, for inspiration and guidance to the superconscious borders of the contact of the material with the spiritual, with the Mystery Monitor."

**ACIM (Manual for Teachers, M 29:6):**
> "Never forget that the Holy Spirit does not depend on your words. He understands the requests of your heart and answers them... God would be cruel if He let your words replace His Own."

**Urantia Book (91:3.7):**
> "Enlightened prayer must recognize not only an external and personal God but also an internal and impersonal Divinity, the indwelling Adjuster."

**Convergence:** Both texts describe prayer as the mechanism by which consciousness reaches upward past ego barriers to contact the divine level. Both say the words of prayer are secondary to the inner intent. ACIM: "the prayer of the heart, not the words." Both identify an inner divine partner — Holy Spirit / Adjuster — as the real recipient. And both describe prayer as having a dual function: looking both to the subconscious (for miracles/material aid) and to the superconscious (for revelation/inspiration).

## X. GOD-CONSCIOUSNESS HAS THREE DIFFERENTIAL LEVELS

**Urantia Book (5:5.11):**
> "God-consciousness, as it is experienced by an evolving mortal of the realms, must consist of three varying factors, three differential levels of reality realization. There is first the mind consciousness — the comprehension of the *idea* of God. Then follows the soul consciousness — the realization of the *ideal* of God. Last, dawns the spirit consciousness — the realization of the *spirit reality* of God."

**ACIM (T 3 E 3-4):**
> "True perception is the BASIS for knowledge, but KNOWING is the affirmation of truth... All of your difficulties ultimately stem from the fact that you do not recognize, or KNOW, yourselves, each other, or God. 'Recognize' means 'know again.'"

**ACIM (T 3 G 14):**
> "Only perception involves partial awareness. Knowledge transcends ALL of the laws which govern perception. Partial KNOWLEDGE is impossible. It is all One, and has no separate parts."

**Convergence:** Both describe a progression from partial awareness (perception/idea) through deeper realization (soul consciousness/true perception) to direct knowing (spirit consciousness/knowledge). Both say the highest level transcends all perception. The UB maps this as idea → ideal → spirit reality. ACIM maps it as perception → true perception → knowledge. Same journey, same architecture.

---

## XI. THE CAUTION: NOT ALL UNCONSCIOUS CONTENT IS DIVINE

**ACIM (T 2 E 25):**
> "You cannot understand unconscious activity in these terms, because 'content' is applicable ONLY to the more superficial unconscious levels to which the individual himself contributes. This is the level at which he can readily introduce fear, and usually does."

**ACIM (T 1B 24f):**
> "If the identification is with the body, consciousness may distort superconscious impulses by denying their Source, & seeking their impact in the orgasm."

**Urantia Book (100:5.6):**
> "The great danger in all these psychic speculations is that visions and other so-called mystic experiences, along with extraordinary dreams, may be regarded as divine communications to the human mind. In times past, divine beings have revealed themselves to certain God-knowing persons, not because of their mystic trances or morbid visions, but in spite of all these phenomena."

**Urantia Book (100:5.10):**
> "Much of the material arising as a result of such preliminary preparation [mystical states] has its origin in the subconscious mind."

**Urantia Book (91:7.4):**
> "The human mind may perform in response to so-called inspiration when it is sensitive either to the uprisings of the subconscious or to the stimulus of the superconscious. In either case it appears to the individual that such augmentations of the content of consciousness are more or less foreign. Unrestrained mystical enthusiasm and rampant religious ecstasy are not the credentials of inspiration, supposedly divine credentials."

**Convergence:** Both texts issue the same critical warning: not everything that surges up from below consciousness is divine. Most of it is the individual's own fear-content. Both specifically caution against mistaking subconscious eruptions for genuine superconscious revelation. Both say genuine divine contact happens *in spite of*, not because of, mystical phenomena.

---

## XII. FEAR OF AWAKENING — THE FUNDAMENTAL RESISTANCE

**ACIM (T 8 I 3):**
> "All forms of sickness, even unto death, are physical expressions of the FEAR OF AWAKENING. They are attempts to reinforce UNCONSCIOUSNESS out of fear of CONSCIOUSNESS."

**ACIM (T 4 D 22):**
> "The calm being of God's Kingdom, which in your sane mind is perfectly conscious, is ruthlessly banished from the part of the mind which the ego rules. The ego is desperate because it opposes literally invincible odds whether you are asleep or awake. Consider how much vigilance you have been willing to exert to protect your ego, and how little you have been willing to exert to protect your higher mind."

**Urantia Book (5:2.5):**
> "It is exceedingly difficult for the meagerly spiritualized, material mind of mortal man to experience marked consciousness of the spirit activities of such divine entities as the Paradise Adjusters. As the soul of joint mind and Adjuster creation becomes increasingly existent, there also evolves a new phase of soul consciousness which is capable of experiencing the presence, and of recognizing the spirit leadings and other supermaterial activities, of the Mystery Monitors."

**Urantia Book (5:3.7):**
> "Sincere worship connotes the mobilization of all the powers of the human personality under the dominance of the evolving soul and subject to the divine directionization of the associated Thought Adjuster. The mind of material limitations can never become highly conscious of the real significance of true worship."

**Convergence:** Both texts describe the fundamental human resistance to higher awareness. ACIM frames it as "fear of awakening" — the ego actively suppresses consciousness of the divine. UB frames it as the material mind's inherent difficulty becoming "highly conscious" of spirit activities. Both say the solution is progressive: as the soul develops (ACIM: right-mindedness grows; UB: soul consciousness evolves), the capacity to receive divine communications increases.

---

## XIII. WORSHIP AS THE FOUR-LEVEL EXPERIENCE

**Urantia Book (5:3.8):**
> "True worship, in the last analysis, becomes an experience realized on four cosmic levels: the intellectual, the morontial, the spiritual, and the personal — the consciousness of mind, soul, and spirit, and their unification in personality."

**ACIM (T 4 D 10):**
> "Against this sense of temporary existence, the Soul offers the knowledge of permanence and unshakeable BEING. No one who has experienced the revelation of this can ever fully believe in the ego again."

**Convergence:** The UB describes worship as engaging all levels simultaneously — mind, soul, spirit, and personality unified. ACIM describes revelation as offering "knowledge of permanence and unshakeable BEING" that permanently alters the relationship with the ego. Both describe the summit experience as one that engages the whole being across all levels of consciousness.
