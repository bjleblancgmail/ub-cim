# Marketing Copy Ideas

## Title
**Bridge: Where the Urantia Book and A Course in Miracles Meet**

---

## The Back Cover (Short)

Two books arrived within a generation. One describes the architecture of the universe. The other trains the mind to experience it. For seventy years, they've been studied apart by two separate communities, each suspicious of the other.

This book puts them in conversation.

What emerges is not compromise. It is recognition. The same Teacher. The same destination. Two languages for one truth.

If you've read one of these books and wondered about the other, start here.

---

## The Amazon Description (Medium)

What if the two most extraordinary spiritual texts of the twentieth century were never meant to be read alone?

The Urantia Book reveals the architecture of reality: a universe teeming with purpose, a God who placed a fragment of himself inside your mind, and a destiny that begins here and ends at the center of all things.

A Course in Miracles trains the mind: 365 daily lessons designed to dissolve fear, release the ego, and restore your awareness of love's presence.

One answers what and why. The other teaches how.

For seventy years, readers of these two revelations have studied them in separate rooms. Urantia Book readers find the Course unsettling. Course students find the Urantia Book overwhelming. Each community carries assumptions about the other that dissolve on contact with the actual texts.

Bridge is the first book to bring them together, passage by passage, concept by concept. What emerges will surprise readers of both: the terminology differs, but the Teacher is the same.

## The Provocative Angle

You have half the picture.

If you study the Urantia Book, you know where you're going. You can describe the mansion worlds, the local universe, the path to Paradise. But when was the last time that knowledge changed how you lived on a Tuesday afternoon?

If you study A Course in Miracles, you know how to change your mind. You practice forgiveness. You hear the Holy Spirit. But do you know what the Holy Spirit actually is? Do you know where forgiveness is taking you?

One book tells you the destination. The other hands you the keys. Bridge shows you they were always meant to travel together.

---

## The Quiet Angle

This is not a book of arguments. It is a book of recognition.

When you place these two texts side by side, something happens that neither produces alone. The Urantia Book's cosmology becomes personal. The Course's mind training becomes cosmic. The gap between knowing and experiencing closes.

If you have ever felt that one of these books was missing something, the other book has it.

---

## The Community Angle

Two million people study the Urantia Book. Three million study A Course in Miracles. Almost none study both.

That is about to change.
