# ACIM High-Density Pentameter Passages

Passages where pentameter-length clauses (9-11 syllables) reach
40%+ of all clauses in a sliding window of 8+ sentences.

Method: Split text on punctuation into clauses, count syllables,
check for 9-11 syllable range. Sliding window finds sustained runs.

Control comparison: Moby Dick overall = 18.8% (flat across book)
ACIM overall = 23.9% (increasing toward conclusion of each volume)
