## Key Observations

1. **The UB is below the normal English prose baseline.** At 13.7%, it's well under Moby Dick's 18.8%. This suggests highly technical, information-dense prose with longer, more complex clause structures.

2. **Part IV (Jesus papers) is the most rhythmic** at 15.2%, approaching but not reaching Moby Dick's level. This makes sense: narrative storytelling tends toward more natural speech rhythms than cosmological exposition.

3. **No within-Part trend.** Unlike ACIM, which builds rhythmically toward each volume's conclusion, the UB is flat within each Part. The Part-to-Part increase is explained by content type (cosmology → narrative).

4. **The UB's highest paper barely matches ACIM's average.** Paper 131 at 21.0% is the UB's peak. ACIM's overall average is 22.2%, and ACIM's peaks reach 28-33%.

5. **Different voices, different rhythms.** The UB reads like technical exposition that occasionally becomes narrative. ACIM reads like verse disguised as prose that becomes more verse-like as it progresses.
